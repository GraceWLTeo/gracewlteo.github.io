Decision Support Platform

Helping instructors evaluate teams with multimodal behavioral data.


Role: Lead Researcher

Methods & Tools: Interviews, Task Analysis, Journey Mapping, Personas, Thematic Analysis, Measurement Framework Development, AI-based tools

Goal

  • Support instructors with objective performance insights.
  • Reduce subjectivity in evaluations.
  • Improve training and team performance.

Problem

  • A single instructor is typically responsible for observing and evaluating an entire team's training session.
  • Evaluations are often subjective, inconsistent, and difficult to standardize.

Process

  • I applied design thinking process to ensure the solution was user-centered.

Phase 1: EMPATHIZE - Understanding how Instructors Decide on Evaluations

  • I conducted task analysis, multiple interviews and observations to understand the instructor-user's workflows, information needs, and decision-making processes.

Key Finding: Instructors Needed Explanations, Not Just Data

  • Research showed that instructors needed to diagnose the causes of performance issues—not just review individual data streams.

  • For example, location data showed what happened, while communication data explained why it happened.

  • This insight led to a key design requirement: synchronize multiple data streams on a shared timeline to support diagnosis and root-cause analysis.

  • The interface aligned location (from GPS), head orientation (from IMUs), and communication transcripts (from audio recordings) within a common timeline, enabling instructors to correlate events and identify contributing factors.

  • A bird's-eye view displayed team members' locations and head orientation throughout the task, while a synchronized communication transcript provided the context needed to interpret team behaviors.

Translating User-defined Evaluation Criteria into Behavioral Measures

  • One challenge was that instructors evaluated abstract concepts such as communication, coordination, and leadership, yet these dimensions were not directly observable in the raw data.

  • Through interviews and observations, I identified the behavioral cues instructors relied on when making these assessments and developed a measurement framework to operationalize them.

  • For example, repeated instructions from a team leader served as an indicator of ineffective communication.

  • These behavioral indicators became a core part of the display, enabling instructors to assess team performance using the same cues they naturally used during evaluation.

  • The decision support tool was interactive and allowed instructors to playback how the team performed.

  • They could go to any timepoint and view where each member was, the direction they were facing, and what was communicated around that timepoint.

  • There was also a "Behavioral Summary" section where instructors could view when specific behavioral cues were observed. These cues aligned with what they would typically look out for when evaluating the team on the evaluation dimensions.

Data Integration and Trust

  • The platform relied on multiple data sources collected from different sensors and systems. To support diagnosis, these data streams needed to be accurately synchronized on a common timeline.

  • Even small timing discrepancies could lead instructors to draw incorrect conclusions about the relationship between behaviors and outcomes.

  • Working closely with engineers, I identified critical points in the data pipeline where synchronization and integration errors could occur and recommended validation checks to verify data quality before integration.

  • These recommendations were estimated to improve data accuracy by 20–30%.

Impact

  • Defined information requirements for instructor workflows
  • Developed a behavioral measurement framework
  • Influenced visualization concepts and information architecture
  • Supported root-cause analysis through synchronized multimodal data views
  • Improved confidence in integrated data through validation recommendations
  • Established a scalable foundation capable of supporting hundreds of instructors
