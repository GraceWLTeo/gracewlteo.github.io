Goals

Identify which team member caused a breakdown, not just that one occurred
Track improvement across sessions for the same team
Run debrief efficiently within a tight schedule
Frustrations
Can't monitor all team members simultaneously during live scenarios
Paper checklists capture outcomes but not the process that led to them
Trainees dispute feedback that isn't backed by objective evidence
No easy way to compare one team's performance to a cohort

Ability to jump to specific moments, not just replaying everything
Helps in problem and error diagnoses
